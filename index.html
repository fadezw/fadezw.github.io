APPLICATION INFORMATION

Personal Video Archive

A personal utility for storing and searching private media backups.

What the application does

The utility uploads media backups and associated transcript files to its owner's Google Drive. It verifies uploaded files and provides the owner with a private way to search transcripts and open recordings at relevant playback times.

Why Google Drive access is requested

Google Drive authorization is used to upload files, read file metadata, verify checksums, and check that archive files are accessible only to the intended account owner. The authorized Drive scope permits broader file access; the utility's archive workflow operates within its designated archive folders.

Who can use it

This is a personal-use application operated for its account owner. This website provides application information only. It does not provide access to the archive, accept uploads, offer a public search service, or sign visitors into Google.
