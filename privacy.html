PERSONAL VIDEO ARCHIVE

Privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Purpose and information processed

Personal Video Archive is a personal-use utility for its account owner. It processes media files, transcripts, file names and other file metadata, checksum and permission information, and the account identity needed to verify the intended Google Drive account. OAuth credentials are used to maintain the owner's authorized connection.

How information is used

Information is used to store private backups, produce and search timed transcripts, verify uploaded files, and check archive access permissions. The utility does not sell this information, use it for advertising, or use Google user data to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Storage and access

The workflow processes data on the owner's devices and private server and stores backups in the owner's Google Drive. Transcription is performed on the owner's server. Archive files and transcript search are not provided through this public information website. Google and infrastructure providers process information as necessary to operate their respective services.

Retention and deletion

Temporary server video copies are removed after the video and transcript uploads have been verified. Transcript files and archive records remain on the server, and the Drive backups remain in the owner's account, until the owner deletes them. Authorized connection credentials are retained to run the utility until removed or revoked.

Control over the Google connection

The account owner can revoke the application's access through their Google Account security settings and can delete backups through Google Drive. Revoking access stops future authorized operations; it does not automatically delete existing backups, server transcripts, or archive records.

This website

This information website contains no sign-in form, archive files, embedded media, analytics scripts, or connection to the private search service. Its hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information under its own privacy practices.

Contact

For questions about this personal utility, use the support contact displayed on its Google authorization screen.
